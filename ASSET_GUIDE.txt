ASSET GUIDE

assets/scenes/   Six wide world-scene images used behind active games
assets/gallery/  Ten world-art images used by cards and visual panels
assets/audio/    Local OGG sound effects and a short synthesized arcade music loop
css/style.css    Visual layout and responsive touch UI
js/data.js       Game card labels, categories, instructions, scene mapping
js/games.js      Canvas renderer, touch input and individual game rules
js/app.js        Catalogue, team score persistence, party mode, sound and navigation
index.html       Website entry point (must stay at project root)

Keep the folder tree intact. Do not upload only index.html by itself.
